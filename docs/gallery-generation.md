# Customer gallery generation record

Created 2026-11-07 with Codex's built-in image-generation workflow. These are fictional demo pets created to show the portrait system; they are not customer commissions or evidence of likeness to a real animal.

## Workflow

Each pet was produced in two stages:

1. an undecorated identity study on warm hanji;
2. a reference-guided integrated portrait that preserved the study's face, silhouette, marking map, pose, and expression while adding one coherent Wish Collection.

The face-crop, silhouette, marking-map, identity-reference comparison, and tradition checks were reviewed before release to the gallery. Generated Korean lettering was excluded; each image retains natural quiet paper for later verified inscription work.
