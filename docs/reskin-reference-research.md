# Minhwa reskin — 50 website references

Research date: **5 November 2026**. This is the first-stage reference pool for the major reskin, not a final design specification. All 50 websites received a desktop browser visit and visual inspection, supplemented by web search and official-page research. Site behavior was sampled, not exhaustively tested.

The strongest direction is **a living painted garden**: large Minhwa paintings, warm paper, botanical framing, a clear editorial structure, and a few small movements. The current project's pet portraits, peonies, lotus, bamboo and butterflies make this a particularly natural fit. This recommendation is our interpretation of the references, not a claim about what those sites implement.

## How to read the list

- **Observed**: the specific change or playback state was seen in the browser. A slideshow is not equivalent to custom motion graphics.
- **Documented**: the artist or credited designer describes the motion; current details were not all reproduced in the browser.
- **Artwork only**: animation/film exists in the artist's work; it does not establish an animated website interface.
- **Unverified**: useful graphic reference, with no confirmed custom motion. This does not mean no motion exists.
- **★** marks the 12 first-visit references above. Supporting candidates are included for materials, layout or cultural vocabulary, rather than presented as equally strong animation showcases.

The folk-art category includes adjacent traditional craft studios, textiles and ceramics because their sites provide stronger material/design references than many institutional folk-art pages. Artist-led studios are identified where relevant. The tea category includes tea brands' official storefronts. Kkokdam and altdif have distinct websites but now belong to the same brand family.

| Desired effect | Closest evidence found | What remains a new design choice |

| Other useful motion | Nature film, process/ritual film, cloud drift, small wildlife movement and artwork transitions. | Choose a small consistent set rather than combining every effect. |

## Proposed next design step

Develop three reviewable directions from this research before implementation:

1. **Living painted garden — recommended.** tashi + SAHCHAS + Jun Sato, anchored in the project's existing Korean Minhwa artwork. Warm paper, large portraits, painted peony/lotus/bamboo borders and one butterfly movement. A paint reveal is an optional prototype, not a dependency for reading the page.
2. **Quiet Korean atelier.** TEA Collective + Magpie & Tiger + Danha + Studio KJD. Ivory, ink and muted botanical color; generous editorial typography; a cinematic process detail; clear portrait creation and gallery actions.
3. **Expressive ink gallery.** Yuko Shimizu + Aoi Yamaguchi + Hekisui + Shantell Martin. A large original brush gesture and confident composition. Use stronger contrast selectively so the pet portraits remain the focus.

For any direction, keep upload, portrait review, approval and order controls visually stable. Use motion mainly in the opening/editorial parts, provide a static reduced-motion version, and make the artwork readable before optional media loads. The reskin should use original project artwork; these are references for composition and behavior, not a source of assets to copy.
