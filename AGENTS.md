# Project Instructions — Korean Minhwa Pet Portraits

Read `PROJECT.md` first and use `GUIDELINE.md` as the complete design-system authority.

## Governing objective

Create commissioned portraits of individual pets that are immediately recognizable while being rendered through an accurate Korean 민화 visual language.

This is first and foremost a portrait project. The pet is the key character, emotional center, and highest-detail subject. Korean traditional painting controls the composition, material language, symbolic environment, palette, spatial logic, and brush treatment.

## Priority hierarchy

1. Individual pet likeness
2. Traditional Korean painting integrity
3. Meaningful symbolism
4. Supporting decoration

If a motif, decorative element, or composition interferes with recognition, change the element and preserve the pet.

## Required workflow

1. Complete or review `templates/customer_intake.md`.
2. Record five to eight recognition anchors from face, full-body, and side/three-quarter references.
3. Produce and approve an undecorated identity study before adding motifs.
4. Run the face-crop, silhouette, marking-map, and familiar-person recognition tests.
5. Translate the selected Wish Collection into an approved motif recipe using `templates/artwork_brief.md`.
6. Keep motifs subordinate in scale, contrast, detail, and placement.
7. Track settings and QA in `templates/production_record.yaml`.
8. Require both the identity gate and tradition gate to pass, with a total QA score of at least 82/100.

## Portrait safeguards

- Preserve exact face geometry, eye spacing and color, ear carriage, muzzle ratio, markings, body proportions, tail, coat direction, characteristic pose, and expression.
- Do not use breed labels as a substitute for individual identity.
- Do not let motifs overlap the ears, eyes, muzzle, defining markings, paws, or tail.
- Keep the pet as the first focal point at thumbnail size.
- Traditionalize through hanji, ink, layered pigment, barim, contour, shallow symbolic space, palette, and rhythm—not by making the pet generic or anatomically inaccurate.
- Do not rely on hanbok, crowns, palaces, temples, or generic pan-Asian decoration to signal Korean tradition.
- Reserve a blank inscription area and add verified Korean calligraphy and the seal in post-production.

## Motif strategy

Customers choose the wish or intention. The studio curates a compatible anchor motif, supporting element, movement element, layout, and palette. Use one coherent Wish Collection and avoid unrelated symbol accumulation.
